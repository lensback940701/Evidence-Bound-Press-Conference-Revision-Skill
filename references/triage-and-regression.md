# Triage and regression reference

Use this reference before applying the evidence-bound press-conference revision skill. A lexical cue is a prompt for contextual reading, never a decision.

## Candidate taxonomy

| code | candidate pattern | usual action | do not remove when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D1 | Self-deprecation or apology | CUT or REFRAME | it states a specific material limitation readers need to interpret a result. |
| D2 | Anticipatory defence against an uninvited objection | CUT, TIGHTEN, or RELOCATE | it is the only sentence preventing a demonstrable over-reading. |
| D3 | Stacked hedge | TIGHTEN to the one hedge that carries the real epistemic limit | multiple hedges mark distinct source, scope, and causal limits. |
| D4 | Work-log narration or failed-attempt chronology | REFRAME as problem, gap, solution, and evidence | the sequence itself is causal evidence or part of the empirical account. |
| D5 | Buried contribution | REFRAME or RELOCATE | the surrounding qualification is a required claim boundary. |
| D6 | Unnecessary comparison or volunteered loss | NARROW, REFRAME, or CUT | comparison names the relevant rival explanation or a substantive trade-off. |
| D7 | Broad negative self-judgment | TIGHTEN to its demonstrated scope | the limitation is a documented finding or method boundary. |
| D8 | Caveat in the wrong rhetorical position | RELOCATE | moving it detaches it from the evidence it qualifies. |
| K1 | Scope condition | KEEP or state once precisely | deleting it broadens the claim. |
| K2 | Source-status distinction | KEEP | it separates plan, authorization, report, observation, outcome, or inference. |
| K3 | Rival, contradiction, delay, reversal, or non-adoption | KEEP | it is data, mechanism, or a live alternative explanation. |
| K4 | Ethical, provenance, reproducibility, or method limitation | KEEP | it is needed for honest interpretation. |

## Three question test

For every candidate, answer in writing:

1. **What function does this wording serve?** Identify rhetorical self-defence, evidence status, scope, method, rival explanation, empirical finding, or more than one.
2. **What would disappear if it were removed?** Name the lost proposition or boundary, not merely a change in tone.
3. **Does the replacement preserve the ceiling?** Confirm that it does not increase certainty, causality, novelty, generality, representativeness, or portability.

If Questions 1–3 cannot be answered confidently, use `QUERY`.

## Correct placement of necessary caution

Keep the caution, but place it where it does its analytical work.

- Keep a source-status distinction next to the source-dependent claim.
- State a general method or scope boundary once in the relevant methods, framework, or discussion location; do not repeat it as an apology in every empirical paragraph.
- Keep a rival explanation next to the inference it qualifies.
- Lead a paragraph with the supported proposition when possible, then place the limit immediately after it rather than replacing the proposition with the limit.

## Regression protocol

Run all checks after each revision batch and again after the full pass.

| check | test | failure response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Claim delta | The new verb and modifier do not exceed the original supported claim. | Revert or query. |
| Evidence status | Plan, authorization, report, observation, outcome, interpretation, and causation remain separate. | Restore the distinction. |
| Citation role | A citation, quotation, date, number, and source-specific wording keep their original support role. | Revert or route to evidence review. |
| Scope | The case, time, population, and comparison boundary remain visible where needed. | Restore or relocate the boundary. |
| Hierarchy | The primary contribution remains primary and secondary lenses remain conditional. | Recast without adding concepts. |
| Section role | Abstract, introduction, framework, evidence, discussion, and conclusion make complementary rather than duplicative moves. | Flag for authorized consistency revision. |
| Voice | The prose is direct and readable without sounding promotional, formulaic, or copied from a comparator. | Tighten locally. |

## Candidate-register interpretation

Use `KEEP` as a positive audit result. A high number of retained cautions is not a failed press-conference revision; it may show that the manuscript has disciplined evidence writing. Assess the concentration of D1–D8 patterns, not the raw number of cue words.

Do not use raw counts as an edit quota or a proof of improvement.
